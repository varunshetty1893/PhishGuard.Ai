# PhishGuard AI — Real-World Benchmark & Generalization Report

**Evaluation Date:** November 2026  
**Methodology:** Zero-leakage, domain-isolated held-out evaluation with Wilson Score 95% Confidence Intervals.

---

## 1. Benchmark Dataset Composition

To prevent data contamination and overly optimistic metrics, PhishGuard AI is evaluated against 4 distinct real-world test sets:

1. **Long-Tail Legitimate Corpus ($n = 1,080$):**
   - Source: Tranco rank 200,000 to 1,000,000.
   - Purpose: Measures false positive rate on legitimate web properties that are NOT globally famous (avoiding bias toward top-100 domains).
   - Composition: 323 URLs with paths and query parameters, 757 root domains.

2. **Authentic Single Sign-On (SSO) & Federation Corpus ($n = 120$):**
   - Source: University portals (Harvard, Oxford, Cambridge, MIT, ETH Zurich), global financial institutions (Barclays, Wells Fargo, Citi, Amex), enterprise IAM (Azure AD, Okta, Ping Identity, Cloudflare Access).
   - Purpose: Stress-tests keyword conditioning (`login`, `auth`, `saml`, `oauth2`) against authentic high-value authentication surfaces.

3. **Hard Phishing Active Feed ($n = 600$):**
   - Source: Live verified phishing campaigns from OpenPhish and URLhaus, collected dynamically.
   - Purpose: Measures real-world zero-day / fresh campaign detection capability.

4. **Held-Out Domain-Isolated Test Split ($n = 7,366$):**
   - Source: Curated multi-source balance, partitioned strictly by apex domain (no domain present in train or validation).

## 3. Prevalence & Operational Impact Analysis

In production email or web proxy environments, phishing URLs are rare relative to legitimate traffic (base rate typically 1% to 5%). High false alarm volume rapidly causes alert fatigue and operational friction.

The table below calculates expected operational precision and alert volume across different operational environments:

| Phishing Prevalence | Production Precision | False Alerts per 1,000 Scanned URLs | True Positives Detected per 1,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.0% (Clean Enterprise)** | 35.4% | 17.3 | 9.5 |
| **5.0% (Medium Risk)** | 74.1% | 16.6 | 47.6 |
| **10.0% (High Exposure / Abuse Inbox)** | 86.1% | 15.8 | 95.1 |
